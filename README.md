# Clevo M ceiling mount — install guide for wood-framed ceilings

**→ [qtyree1328.github.io/clevo-ceiling-mount](https://qtyree1328.github.io/clevo-ceiling-mount/)**

An interactive 3D guide to mounting a [CLEVO M climbing ceiling module](https://clevo-climbing.com/en/products/clevo-m-climbing-ceiling-module)
into a drywalled ceiling on wooden joists, rather than the concrete slab it is designed for.

The module ships with concrete anchors and expects you to drill wherever you like. A joisted
ceiling gives you solid wood only every 16 inches, and the mounting holes are 27⅛ inches apart,
so they never line up. The fix is blocking between the joists plus through-bolts, and this page
works out exactly where each of the eight holes lands.

## The numbers that matter

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Bolt lines, rail to rail | **689 mm / 27⅛″** |
| Holes per rail | **4**, at 80.67 mm / 3³⁄₁₆″ pitch |
| Hole span per rail | 242 mm / 9½″ |
| Flange hole diameter | **11.4 mm** — a ½″ bolt will not pass |
| Bolts | ⅜″ × 7″ grade 5, or M10 × 180 class 8.8 |

The published spec sheet gives the module's length as 660 mm, which is the outside of the two
steel plates. The mounting flanges fold outward past them, so the holes sit 689 mm apart. If you
have measured 27″ with a tape and doubted it against the spec, you measured correctly.

Read off the dimensioned drilling template on p. 14 of the CLEVO M user manual and cross-checked
against the manufacturer's CAD model.

## Using it

Open the page. Everything is in one file — no build step, no server.

- **Bolt layout** switches between three ways of positioning the unit across the joists. The first is the one to build; the other two show why they are worse.
- **Exploded view** separates the layers.
- **Units** toggles inch/mm throughout, including the drill plan.
- The drill plan below the viewer is to scale and dimensioned.

## Read this before you drill

The framing dimensions throughout are **one specific house** — 2x6 joists actual 5⅜″ × 1½″, bays
14″ and 14⅜″ clear, ½″ drywall, attic access above. Yours will differ. Measure your own.
The 689 × 242 mm hole pattern is the only number here that is the same for every Clevo M.

Clevo specify concrete or solid timber ceilings and accept no responsibility for how or where the
unit is fixed. A drywalled joist ceiling is neither. The detail here is sound, but it sits outside
what they warrant, and you are hanging bodyweight plus dynamic load overhead. If anything about
your framing is unclear — particularly the joist clear span — pay a carpenter or structural
engineer to look at it.
